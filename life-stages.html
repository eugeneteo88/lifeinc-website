Life stages

A plan that grows up alongside you.

The right money moves look different at every chapter — your first pay cheque, a wedding, a new baby, the keys to a home, and the day work becomes optional.

Book a free review →

For every chapter of your life.

Wherever you are right now, we meet you there — and plan a few steps ahead.

1Starting out

Your first pay cheque.

New job, real income, and a lot of freedom. The habits you build now are the ones that quietly compound for the next thirty years.

  • ✓Set aside an emergency fund — three to six months of expenses.
  • ✓Get basic health & hospitalisation cover while it's cheap and you're healthy.
  • ✓Start investing early — time is the biggest advantage you'll ever have.
Talk it through →
2Getting married

Two lives, one plan.

You're combining finances, dreams and responsibilities. For the first time, someone else is counting on the choices you make.

  • ✓Review each other's existing cover and find the gaps.
  • ✓Make sure you're both protected if one income disappears.
  • ✓Plan together for the big goals — a home, a family, travel.
Talk it through →
3A growing family

Little ones who count on you.

A new arrival changes everything — and your responsibilities grow overnight. This is the stage where protection matters most.

  • ✓Income protection so the family is safe if you can't work.
  • ✓Life & critical-illness cover sized to the people who depend on you.
  • ✓Start an education plan early, while small amounts still add up.
Talk it through →
4Buying a home

The keys to your own place.

Your biggest commitment yet — a mortgage that can run for decades. The goal is simple: make sure the home stays in the family, no matter what.

  • ✓Cover the mortgage so your home is safe if something happens to you.
  • ✓Rebalance your protection around this bigger commitment.
  • ✓Keep some liquidity — don't leave yourself cash-tight after the deposit.
Talk it through →
5Building your wealth

Hitting your stride.

Mid-career, earning more, thinking longer-term. Now it's about making your money work as hard as you do — without taking your eye off the risks.

  • ✓Check your cover still fits a bigger life and bigger commitments.
  • ✓Grow investments with a plan that matches your goals and comfort with risk.
  • ✓Guard against a critical illness quietly derailing your progress.
Talk it through →
6Retiring well

Time that's finally yours.

The finish line of work is the starting line of enjoying it. The question shifts from "how do I grow it?" to "how do I make it last?"

  • ✓Turn savings into steady retirement income you won't outlive.
  • ✓Plan for healthcare costs in your later years.
  • ✓Organise your legacy so what you've built passes on the way you intend.
Talk it through →
